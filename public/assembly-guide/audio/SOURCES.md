# Befun assembly contact effects

These are edited illustrative contact sounds, not recordings of OVVO or Blum hardware tests.

- **Impact Sounds 1.0**, Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds — Creative Commons Zero (CC0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Source files: `impactWood_heavy_000.ogg`, `impactWood_light_000.ogg`, `impactMetal_light_000.ogg` from the author's official ZIP.
- **Mechanical Plastic Click 09**, SmallConfusion, https://freesound.org/people/SmallConfusion/sounds/751087/ — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Public HQ preview used. The author describes a 50ms recording of half a clothespin hitting a phone.

Edits by Befun: remove leading silence, shorten tails, high/low-pass EQ, fade, mix and peak-match the final samples. No oscillator or melodic tone added. All delivered files are mono 48kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Final peaks: wood/connector 0.28, spring 0.20, hinge 0.26; the player further lowers preparation/spring/door-closing events.

| Delivered file | Sources and changes |
|---|---|
| wood-seat.wav | Kenney wood heavy, low-pass 1800Hz, 220ms maximum, gain 0.50 |
| connector-seat.wav | SmallConfusion mechanical click (gain 0.58) + Kenney wood light low body (gain 0.22), 160ms maximum |
| spring-lock.wav | SmallConfusion click, high-pass 180Hz / low-pass 4200Hz, gain 0.38, 75ms maximum |
| hinge-lock.wav | SmallConfusion click (gain 0.58) + shortened Kenney metal light (gain 0.10), 120ms maximum |

Source access/edits: 2026-10-14. Attribution is also linked from the simulator's model/assembly information.
