

# Troubleshooting Inserts That Don't Fit

Source: https://tylko.elevio.help/en/articles/35-troubleshooting-inserts-that-don-t-fit | Saved: 2026-10-14

In this article you will find tips on how to solve issues with inserts (elements marked with the letter I). 

If you cannot assemble the insert, it could be due to a production error from incorrect drilling or connector placement on an insert, or on a vertical or horizontal element. We will describe how to measure and check the elements step by step to determine if this is the case.

Checking the Position of Connectors

There are connectors shaped like a figure eight on both the left and right sides of the insert, with distinct top and bottom directions. The direction is important for correct installation, so observe their placement on the left and right sides, viewing from the front.

Right Side: The opening in which the insert will be placed should have the connectors orientated like so when viewed from the front.

If a figure eight connector is mounted differently than in the picture above, rotate the element vertically (top to bottom). You'll find the instruction on how to do this [here](https://tylko.com/en-uk/faq/articles/self-assembly-disassembly/how-do-i-disassemble-my-tylko).

If a figure eight connector is mounted the same way as shown above yet you are still experiencing difficulty, please check the measurements detailed in the next step. If the pins do not fit into the connectors, try flipping the insert upside-down (vertically) before assembly. If this doesn't help, the next step is to measure a few distances on the inner shelf as well as on the horizontals and verticals to confirm whether their placement is correct.

On the inner shelf, measure the spacing from the centre of the pins to the edge of the element (as shown in the diagram).

![Assembly diagram](../images/046_BP_zly_rozmiar_ABCD_01-cbU.png)

On horizontal and vertical elements, measure the distance from the edge to the middle of the figure eight-shaped connector (as shown in the diagram).

The distance from the back edge to the middle of the figure eight connector

Right Side Element:

The distance from the back edge to the middle of the figure eight connector
